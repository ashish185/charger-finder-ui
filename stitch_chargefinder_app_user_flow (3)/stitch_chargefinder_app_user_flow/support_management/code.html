help

How do I reconcile monthly discrepancies?

Navigate to the Discrepancies tab and use the 'Batch Resolve' feature for all verified hardware logs. Official reports are generated every 1st of the month.

network_check

Recommended OCPP version?

Chargefinder currently fully supports OCPP 1.6J and 2.0.1. We recommend 2.0.1 for all new station installations to ensure smart-charging compatibility.

payments

Payout cycle schedules?

CPO payouts are processed bi-weekly. Funds are transferred to your registered business bank account after a 7-day holding period for validation.
